# Formally (Prototype)

## Summary

An early-stage fitness and personal development platform covering programs, progress
tracking, and community features. The repository is private while the product is still in
development.

## The problem

Fitness apps front-load configuration. Before they give you anything useful they want your
goals, your equipment, your schedule, and your measurements, and a lot of people quit
during setup rather than during training. The prototype tested how much of that could be
deferred or dropped entirely without the resulting plan getting worse.

## What is implemented

- Authentication and authorization with JWT and role-based access, so admin and member
  views can diverge safely
- Programs, progress tracking, goals, and notifications
- Real-time messaging and group chats over Socket.io
- An admin panel, with English and Turkish language support

## What came out of it

The component library built here became the starting point for how I structure interfaces
in later projects. Getting the reusable pieces right once, then reaching for them
repeatedly, is most of what makes a small team ship quickly.

## Tech

React on the frontend, Node.js and Express on the backend, MongoDB with Mongoose, Socket.io,
Tailwind, and Docker.
