# Bloom

**Live app:** [bloom-cal.vercel.app](https://bloom-cal.vercel.app) · Sign in with Google.

## Summary

Bloom is a planner that holds calendar, tasks, habits, notes, journal, goals, and a focus
timer in one place. I designed and built it solo. It is live now, and a public launch on
web, iOS, and Android is in progress, which is why the repository is private for the
moment.

## The problem

Planner apps tend to fall into two groups. Some sync reliably and feel like enterprise
software. Others feel good to use and quietly lose your data between devices. I wanted one
that syncs properly, works with no connection, and does not make you feel bad for missing
a day.

## The engineering I care about: sync you never notice

Bloom is offline-first. Every write lands in local state immediately through Zustand and
localStorage, then syncs to Postgres on a debounce in the background. The interface never
waits on a network round trip, so it stays responsive on bad transit Wi-Fi.

Realtime subscriptions push changes back out to every signed-in device. Add a task on your
phone on the bus and it is already on your laptop when you sit down. Theme and settings
travel with the account too, not just content.

- **Auth:** Google sign-in through Supabase Auth.
- **Data:** PostgreSQL, 10 tables, each protected by Row Level Security. Accounts are
  isolated at the database level rather than by client-side checks, so a bug in the
  frontend cannot expose another user's data.
- **Offline:** PWA with Workbox. The app keeps working with no connection and reconciles
  when it returns.
- **Safety:** Markdown notes are sanitized with DOMPurify before render.

## Product decisions

**Forgiveness-based habit scoring.** Streak counters reset to zero the first day life gets
in the way, which is exactly when people quit. Bloom uses a 0 to 100% consistency score
that dips instead of resetting. It is kinder and it is a more accurate description of how
someone is actually doing.

**Mood and habit correlation.** Log a daily mood and the statistics view shows how it moves
alongside your habits over 30 days.

**Speed as a feature.** A global Ctrl/Cmd+K creates a task, event, or note from any screen
in about two seconds. If capture is slow, people stop capturing.

**A focus timer that follows you.** Start a Pomodoro and a small widget keeps counting as
you move around the app, with lo-fi, rain, or fireplace audio.

## Tech

- React 18 + TypeScript (Vite)
- Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion
- Zustand for state, persisted to localStorage
- Supabase: PostgreSQL, Google Auth, Realtime
- PWA via Workbox, deployed on Vercel

## Why it matters

Bloom is the project where I am both the product designer and the engineer. I decide what
deserves to exist and then build it: the data model, the sync engine, authentication,
theming, and every screen. It is also where I practice shipping like a product team rather
than a student, which means a private beta first, then polish, then a public launch.
